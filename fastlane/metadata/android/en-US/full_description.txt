<b>HomeFeed</b> is a clean, modern, and highly customizable RSS and Atom feed widget for your Android home screen. Stay informed with news, articles, and updates from your favorite sources without constantly opening apps.

<b>Key Features:</b>

• <b>Multi-Feed Support:</b> Aggregate multiple RSS and Atom feeds into a single, unified stream on your home screen.
• <b>Distraction-Free Reader:</b> Read article content instantly in an integrated reader popup, or open full articles in your browser or Chrome Custom Tabs.
• <b>Rich Media & Thumbnails:</b> Automatically extracts high-resolution article images and media enclosures.
• <b>Material You Design:</b> Full support for Material 3 and dynamic theming, automatically adapting to your system wallpaper and dark/light mode.

<b>Customization & Layout:</b>

• <b>Layout Modes:</b> Choose between full image cards or a space-efficient Compact Mode with customizable thumbnail positioning (left or right).
• <b>Typography & Colors:</b> Pick your preferred font family (Sans-serif, Serif, Monospace) and customize title and description text colors to suit your home screen aesthetic.
• <b>Widget Appearance:</b> Adjust background opacity, corner radius, item spacing, and header visibility.
• <b>Article Controls:</b> Toggle descriptions, trim length, or show article source labels.

<b>Reading Management:</b>

• <b>Read Status Filtering:</b> Convenient in-widget header control lets you toggle between showing, dimming, or hiding read articles.
• <b>Tap-to-Read:</b> Track what you've read effortlessly with automatic status updates.

<b>Smart Sync & Performance:</b>

• <b>Battery-Friendly Sync:</b> Set automatic background refresh intervals and define active hours to prevent syncing while you sleep.
• <b>Quick Manual Refresh:</b> On-demand refresh button right on the widget header.
• <b>Privacy-First & Offline:</b> Feeds and articles are cached locally on your device with zero tracking and minimal resource usage.
